# Su-25 for Nuclear Option

Exterior, cockpit and instruments adapted from FGMEMBERS/Su-25:
https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/Su-25

Original aircraft credits: Petar Jedvaj, Stuart Cassie, Raptor and Gary Brown.
The model XML specifically credits Petar Jedvaj for the exterior and instruments.
Source license: GNU GPL v3, retained in LICENSE.txt. Original editable AC3D
models and textures are preserved in SourceAssets/Su-25-model-source.zip.

Changes: converted AC3D geometry to OBJ/MTL, retained separate named parts and
corner UVs, transformed axes for the Unity workflow. This is an unfinished
adaptation, not an official aircraft or an endorsement by the original authors.

Blueprinter editor: https://github.com/nikkorap/NOBlueprinter-Editor
Nuclear Option game assets are local development dependencies and must not be
included in redistributed mod packages.
